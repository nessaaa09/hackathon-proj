About WaterWatch

Stronger communities. Safer water.

WaterWatch connects residents and local water officials. Residents report shortages and contamination, officials verify them, and everyone can see what is being done on a shared map.

This is a starter version. Data is saved in your browser only, so you can build on it with a real backend later.
